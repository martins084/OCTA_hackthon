    Drivers in the portfolio

    Every recorded trip scored by the same engine. The policy factor weights each trip by distance. Premiums use one identical driver and vehicle profile, so the differences you see come from driving alone.

    Tier A ≥ 85 · B 75–85 · C 60–75 · D 45–60 · E < 45. Telematics factor 0.7–1.75, equal to 1.0 at score 75. Base price €120 at the portfolio-average claim frequency (illustrative). “Seen in training” means the style model learned from this driver, so those scores are optimistic.
